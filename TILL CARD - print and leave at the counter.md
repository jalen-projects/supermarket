# MAQAM FOOD CITY SUPERMARKET
## The till — everything a cashier needs

**Print this. Leave one copy at every till.**

---

## STARTING YOUR DAY

1. Open the system in the browser (the shortcut on the desktop).
2. Sign in **with your own name and password**. Never use somebody else's.
3. If the manager gave you change money, open **Cash up (hand over)** and record
   it under *change money in the drawer*.

> **Why your own login matters:** your name prints on every receipt you serve, and
> your drawer is counted against **your** sales only. Sharing a login means
> somebody else's shortage becomes yours.

---

## SELLING — the whole job

| Step | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Scan** each item. No barcode? Type part of the name and press **Enter**. |
| 2 | Same item twice? Scan it twice, or type the quantity in the box. |
| 3 | Read the big **TOTAL**. That is what the customer owes. |
| 4 | Type what they handed you into **Cash received** — or tap **Exact money**, or tap the notes (10,000 then 5,000) and it adds up. |
| 5 | **Change** turns green — that is what you hand back. Red means they have not given enough. |
| 6 | Press **CASH OUT** (or **F9**). |

That one button takes the money, prints the receipt, and removes the goods from
stock. **There is nothing else to press.**

### Handy keys
- **F2** — jump back to the barcode box
- **F9** — cash out

---

## WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

**The receipt did not print.**
The sale is saved. Look for the green line under the button and click
*Open the receipt again*.

**The system says the item is expired.**
It will not sell it, and that is deliberate. Take it off the shelf and tell the
manager.

**The system says there is not enough stock.**
What is on the shelf does not match the books. Sell what you can and tell the
manager — do not guess.

**The customer wants to cancel after you cashed out.**
Only the manager can void a sale. Call them. Do not try to fix it by selling
something at a strange price.

**The screen will not load / it says it cannot connect.**
The main computer has been switched off or has gone to sleep.
**Do not switch anything off.** Call the manager.

---

## ENDING YOUR SHIFT — cash up

**Do this before you hand over the money. Every time.**

1. Open **Cash up (hand over)**.
2. **Count everything in the drawer** — every note and every coin, *including*
   the change money you started with.
3. Do **not** take anything out first. Do **not** subtract anything.
4. Type the total and press **Hand over the drawer**.
5. Print the sheet. Hand it over **with** the money.

> Took money out during the shift — paid a boda for a delivery, gave the manager
> cash? **Write it in the note box.** That is exactly what it is for.

> Mobile money and credit sales are **not** expected in the drawer. The system
> knows. You are only ever answerable for the **cash**.

---

## THE RULES, SHORT

- ✅ Sign in as yourself, always
- ✅ Cash up at the end of every shift
- ✅ Every item goes through the till, including the small ones
- ❌ Never share your password
- ❌ Never leave the till signed in when you walk away
- ❌ Never switch off the main computer
