Bring the shop's existing list in from a spreadsheet

This is for the first day, when everything the shop already sells has to get onto the system. Instead of typing every product, put the list in a spreadsheet and upload it here. Nothing is saved until you have seen exactly what will happen.

  1. Download the stock entry sheet and fill it in - it stops Excel from damaging barcodes, dates and numbers - or use a list you already have.
  2. Save it in Excel as normal and upload the Excel file itself (.xlsx). That keeps every barcode exact - saving as CSV makes Excel round long barcodes into numbers like 6.00962E+12, and they can no longer be scanned. A CSV still works.
  3. Upload it here and read the check report before committing.
