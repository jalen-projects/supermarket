Doing this well

  • Start with what sells fastest, not with shelf number one. The shop can open and trade on two hundred fast movers while the rest is still being captured.
  • Work one category at a time. The category box keeps its value, so a whole shelf goes in without re-picking it.
  • No barcode? Leave it blank. Loose goods are found by typing the name at the till.
  • Scanning something already captured is fine - it tops up the quantity instead of complaining.
  • Anything that is not yet captured cannot be sold. Capture as you go, and take a backup before you stop for the day.
