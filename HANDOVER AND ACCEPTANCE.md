## 4. WHAT THE OWNER IS RESPONSIBLE FOR FROM TODAY

- [ ] **Taking a backup every trading evening** and keeping the flash disk
      **away from the shop**. A backup kept on the counter does not survive a theft.
- [ ] Keeping the **server computer switched on** during trading hours.
- [ ] Giving **each cashier their own login**. Sharing one login destroys the
      cash-up and the "served by" record on every receipt.
- [ ] **Reading the cash-up sheet** at every handover, and asking about any
      drawer that is short or that has voided sales.
- [ ] Continuing to **capture the remaining stock**.
